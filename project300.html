CURSED GROATH

Parter: Phil Warren

27 July, 2025 @SCI-Arc South Gallery



"When a chicken becomes a meal, it is of course dead."

Yet when the meal is still in the form of its raw material, a piece of chicken thigh for example, it somehow still feels alive. Where does this lingering sense of life come from? Perhaps it lies in the aesthetic qualities of rawness itself: in the primal and visceral materiality that resists full abstraction. This thesis is a curation of Grand Central Market into an theatrical experience that reminds forms of desire that touches the primal.
